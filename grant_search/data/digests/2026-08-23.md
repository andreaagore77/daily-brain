Still nothing approved, so no application advanced. This run went at the two

## Champions in Action is better than rated — moved 12% → 18%

Three things confirmed today, all favourable:

**Budget eligibility — the organization qualifies.** Citizens requires an
operating budget between **$250,000 and $5 million**. At $300,000 Clear
Conscience Consulting clears the floor. That was previously unknown and could
easily have gone the other way.

**The 2026 theme is broad and favourable.** It is *"Uplifting Resilient
Nonprofits"* — organizations adapting and innovating to meet community needs.
The earlier worry that a cause-area theme might exclude behavioral health does
not apply; this theme is about organizational character, not service line.

**The award is more than money.** $50,000 plus volunteer engagement and
promotional support from Citizens.

**But there is a real catch, and it is operational.** Applicants must be able to
host **one to two volunteer projects for Citizens employees, at least one of
them skills-based.** That is a commitment, not a formality. Given the
confidentiality needs of a justice-involved clientele, anything client-facing
is likely unworkable — a skills-based project using corporate volunteers for
finance, HR, technology, or marketing support is the realistic shape. Worth
deciding whether the organization can host that before applying.

Net: Champions in Action rises to 18% and moves into tier B. With Empower New
Jersey's scope now in question from yesterday, these two may have swapped
positions — both are 2026-09-30, and if only one gets written, Champions now
has the better-established case.

## HFNJ — programmatic fit confirmed strong

Behavioral health constitutes the **majority** of HFNJ's non-hospital
grantmaking, and it awarded roughly **$2.2–2.3M to Newark hospitals and
behavioral health in Q4 2025 alone**. This is a funder that actively wants to
fund this kind of work.

The one open question stands: no source states explicitly whether a
non-Federation-affiliated organization can apply under the greater Newark
track. The framing suggests greater Newark is served broadly, but it is worth a
direct call to HFNJ rather than an inference. Deadline is 2026-11-19, so there
is time.
