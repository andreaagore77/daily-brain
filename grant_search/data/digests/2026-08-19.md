# Grant digest — 2026-08-19

Two runs today: an initial web search, then a triage of the Instrumentl
opportunity-match export supplied by the org. The export supersedes the search
results — it carries real deadlines and amounts, which the sandbox's blocked
egress prevented the search from verifying.

**Correction:** the earlier run recorded HFNJ Standard Grants as closing
Aug 28. The export shows **Nov 19**. The Aug 28 date came from an unverified
search snippet and was wrong.

Of ~80 listed opportunities: **5 to act on now, 16 strong fits with later
deadlines, 7 needing research, ~26 screened out.**

## Act now

**Two Second Chance Act solicitations, both 2026-09-24, both up to $1M.**
*Improving Reentry Education and Employment Outcomes* and *Family-Based
Substance Use Disorder Treatment*. These are the closest federal matches to the
mission that exist — the first is reentry plus education/employment, the second
hits SUD, family, and justice involvement simultaneously. Nonprofit
community-based organizations are eligible, and the NJ recovery court, IDRC,
and reentry partnerships are exactly the local infrastructure these
solicitations expect.

Two cautions. A $1M award is over three times annual budget; federal reviewers
weigh organizational capacity heavily, and a sub-award under a larger lead
applicant may score better than a solo bid. And the Family-Based one is titled
a *treatment* program — the org does psychoeducation and wraparound and refers
out to outpatient/IOP. Confirm the NOFO accepts a treatment-partner structure
before committing writing time.

**CFNJ Empower New Jersey — 2026-09-30, $50,000–$100,000.** Best
effort-to-probability ratio on the whole list. Statewide, award size
proportionate to the org (17–33% of annual budget), and a foundation process
rather than a federal one. If only one thing gets written this fall, this is
the one.

**Citizens Charitable Foundation Champions in Action — 2026-09-30, $50,000.**
Flat award; check this cycle's theme.

**HFNJ Standard Grants — 2026-11-19.** Three of four priority counties are in
footprint. Still open: whether a non-Federation-affiliated org qualifies under
the "vulnerable populations elsewhere in Essex, Morris, and Union" clause.
(HFNJ *Renewal* Grants share the Nov 19 date but are for existing grantees —
not applicable unless previously funded.)

## Screened out and why

- **Research institution required (~9):** every NIH mechanism on the list
  (R61/R33, P50, P30, K12, R25, U01), the CDC research cooperative agreements,
  PCORI, BJS CHRARP, NJ Brain Injury Research, DoD/Army TBI. These need a PI,
  an academic home, and IRB infrastructure.
- **State/government applicant only (~9):** 988 Administrator ($231M), Disaster
  Distress Helpline, Comprehensive Suicide Prevention for States, SSUS STD/HIV,
  LINCS, Cancer Control, Diabetes, Rural Health Transformation.
- **National TTA contracts (~5):** Drug Court TTA ($6M), RSAT TTA, both CSBG
  programs (which route through designated Community Action Agencies).
- **Geography/topic mismatch (3):** the rural opioid programs (the four
  priority counties aren't rural), adult literacy programs, and the Affordable
  Housing Demonstration (housing-provider focus, and five days out).

## Read on this

The federal picture improved sharply. This morning's search found the two
best-fit federal programs already closed; the export shows BJA reopened both
Second Chance Act tracks with a Sep 24 deadline. That's the headline.

Realistically, though, the near-term win is CFNJ Empower NJ — right size, right
geography, achievable process. The Second Chance Act applications are worth
pursuing but are heavy lifts at this budget scale, and are stronger as
partnership bids than solo ones.
