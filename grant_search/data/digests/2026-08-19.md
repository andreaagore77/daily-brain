First run. 5 open/possible opportunities recorded, 5 expired ones filtered out,
4 recurring programs flagged to watch.

## ⚠️ Read this before acting on anything below

This sandbox has outbound HTTP egress blocked for everything except the search
tool itself. Direct calls to the Grants.gov API and page fetches of
samhsa.gov, hfnj.org, cfnj.org, and simpler.grants.gov were all refused by the
network proxy. **Every item below comes from search-result summaries, not from
the funder's own page.** Deadlines and dollar figures in search snippets are
frequently stale. Confirm at the source URL before building any application
plan around a date here.

## Act on now

**Healthcare Foundation of New Jersey — Standard Grants ($35,000+)**
Deadline **2026-08-28 — 9 days out**. Next cycle after that is Nov 21.
HFNJ funds at-risk/underserved health programs across greater Newark and the
Greater MetroWest footprint — reportedly Essex, Morris, Somerset, Union, parts
of Sussex. That overlaps three of the four priority counties, and recent
priorities reportedly emphasize youth and behavioral health. Best fit found.
→ https://hfnj.org/applications-deadlines/

Two things to check first: whether the Aug 28 date still holds, and whether an
org with no Jewish Federation affiliation qualifies under the "targeted
vulnerable populations elsewhere in Essex, Morris, and Union" clause. If Aug 28
is too tight, the same funder's **Special One-time Grants** (under $35,000) are
rolling, and are a lower-friction way to open the relationship.

## Open, rolling

- **Community Foundation of NJ small grants** — up to $15,000, rolling,

## Needs verification

- **Horizon Foundation for NJ — 2026 Arts & Community Health RFP.** Released
  June 1, decisions December, programs start Jan 2027. No deadline surfaced in
  search. Two caveats: the "Arts &" framing may not fit a pure behavioral
  health program, and it reportedly requires a current CPA financial audit
  from *all* applicants including those under $500k income — a real expense
  against a $300k budget.
- **NJ DOL Pathways to Recovery** — $5M for services to residents impacted by
  opioid use; nonprofits tied to MH/OUD treatment centers eligible, which your
  treatment partnerships would support. Fall pre-application reportedly opened
  mid-June; unclear whether still open.

## Watch list for next cycle

The two best-fit federal programs both closed months ago:

- **SAMHSA Treatment, Recovery, and Workforce Support** — $10.5M, up to $500k
  per award, 21 awards, open to nonprofits providing SUD/COD treatment and
  recovery in partnership with local/state stakeholders.
- **BJA Second Chance Act, Community-Based Reentry** — $500k–$750k, explicitly
  open to nonprofit community-based reentry organizations. On fit alone this is
  probably the single best federal match for the mission.

Also worth noting: **NJLEAD** awarded 41 organizations on July 6. It funds
municipalities to hire Reentry Coordinators, so the realistic route is becoming
a service provider to a funded municipality rather than applying directly —
that's a partnership conversation, not a grant application, and it doesn't have
to wait for a cycle to reopen.

## Honest read on this run

The federal calendar worked against us: the two closest-fitting programs
(SAMHSA TI-26-009, Second Chance Act) closed in May and July. What's actually
actionable right now is the New Jersey foundation layer, where HFNJ is a strong
geographic and programmatic match with a near-term date.
