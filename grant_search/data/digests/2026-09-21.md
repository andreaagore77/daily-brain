## Champions in Action may have closed in March

Search today indicates the 2026 Champions in Action cycle **closed 2026-03-13**,
and that the 2026 theme was **"Building AI Readiness and Capacity in
Nonprofits"** — not "Uplifting Resilient Nonprofits," which is what this system
recorded on 2026-08-23 and used to upgrade the grant from 12% to 18%.

If that is right, this opportunity has been closed since March, and the
2026-09-30 date from the Instrumentl export is wrong.

**For three consecutive weeks this system called Champions in Action the best
remaining target and the last comfortably reachable item on the board.** That
advice may have been pointing at a closed opportunity the entire time. The
upgrade rested on a theme that appears to belong to a different cycle, and the
date was never independently confirmed — it came from the export and was
repeated with increasing confidence rather than increasing evidence.

**What still holds:** the budget band ($250k–$5M), New Jersey among eligible
locations, the volunteer-hosting requirement, and the $50,000 unrestricted
award. Eligibility was established soundly. Timing and theme were not.

**What to do:** open citizensbank.com/champions directly. Nine days is enough to
apply but not enough to waste, and this needs the funder's own page rather than
another search — search has been wrong on this pipeline before, giving a false
HFNJ date in August and contradicting the Second Chance Act date eight times
while the export turned out correct.

## The pattern worth naming

Every near-term recommendation this system has made ultimately turned on a
funder page it cannot open. Looking back across five weeks:

- Search established **eligibility facts** reliably — budget bands, applicant
  categories, geographic footprints, the CSBG restriction, NJDOL's
  reimbursement basis. Those findings held up.
- Search established **dates and cycles** unreliably — a wrong HFNJ deadline, an
  eight-search failure on Second Chance Act, and now a possible three-week error
  on Champions.

Any date in this record not confirmed at source should be treated as
provisional, including ones this system has stated confidently.
