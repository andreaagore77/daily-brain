## Scope detail worth knowing before drafting

The Family-Based program is **narrower than "family services"**. It targets
pregnant parents, or parents of minor children, who have a substance use
disorder and are under correctional control — in community settings, jail,
prison, or as an alternative to incarceration.

A competitive application would need to show the organization serves
justice-involved *parents of minor children* specifically, not family-focused
programming in general. That is a real question about the current caseload, and
one only the organization can answer.
