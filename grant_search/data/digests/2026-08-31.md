# Grant digest — 2026-08-31

First weekly run. The longest-running open question is resolved — and resolving
it opened a more consequential one.

## The Second Chance Act deadline is real: Sept 24, then Oct 1

Eight searches across two weeks could not establish this. It surfaced today
through a **DOJ announcement dated 2026-08-24**: $211 million across five grants
covering reentry, substance use, crisis response and behavioral health, with
deadlines spanning September 24 to October 2.

**2026-09-24 is the Grants.gov deadline. JustGrants closes 2026-10-01**, a week
later — confirming the paired-deadline pattern flagged on the 22nd, though the
gap is a week rather than the two to three days seen elsewhere.

The Instrumentl export was correct all along. Worth noting for calibration: the
export beat eight rounds of search, and the two occasions this system doubted it
were both wrong.

## But the org may not be able to apply to that track at all

The FY26 **Improving Reentry Education and Employment Outcomes** program is
described as being for **state and local governments**, with awards allocated by
county type — up to $1.6M each for 24 urban counties, $1.3M each for 26 suburban
counties, $1M each for 13 rural counties.

That is a county-allocation structure, not an open nonprofit competition. This
record previously said nonprofit community-based organizations were eligible;
that came from the FY25 cycle and may not carry into FY26.

**If that holds, Clear Conscience Consulting cannot be the applicant of record
on this track.** The route becomes subrecipient or named partner on an Essex,
Bergen, Union or Somerset county application — which is no longer just the
better strategy, it is the only one. And it is time-sensitive in a way a solo
application would not be: a county needs to know you exist before it finalises
its own submission, which means approaching them this week, not in three.

The Family-Based SUD track looks different — O-BJA-2026-172700 was posted
2026-07-16 with an explicit nonprofit Category 2, so that one does appear open
directly.

**Confirm both at `bja.ojp.gov/funding/current`.**

## The one thing worth doing this week

If the county-partner reading is right, the highest-value action available is a
phone call to a county government — not a grant application. Counties applying
for reentry education money need community-based service providers with court
and IDRC relationships, which is precisely what this organization has and what a
county does not. That is a strong position to approach from, and it expires on
2026-09-24.
