# Grant digest — 2026-09-07

Eighth run. Nothing approved, no folders, 106 placeholders. What has changed is
the calendar: deadlines that were comfortable are now close, and some are past
the point where a credible submission is possible.

This digest is triage rather than research.

## Still comfortably achievable

**Champions in Action — 2026-09-30, 23 days.** This is now the best target on
the board, and one new detail today makes it better: the $50,000 is
**unrestricted**, plus local promotion and volunteer support. Unrestricted money
matters disproportionately at this budget size — it funds the infrastructure
restricted project grants never cover — and only two items in the entire
pipeline offer it.

Everything about this one is confirmed: budget eligibility ($250k–$5M, the org
qualifies), a broad 2026 theme, NJ footprint. The only open item is the
volunteer-hosting requirement, which is a decision rather than a barrier. A
foundation-scale application in 23 days is comfortable.

## Achievable only if started immediately

**Empower New Jersey — 2026-09-30, 23 days.** Same window, but the first step is
not drafting. The economic-empowerment scope question raised on the 22nd is
still unverified, and five minutes at cfnj.org decides whether this is worth
writing at all.

**SNAP STEPS — 2026-09-12, 5 days.** Only a Letter of Intent, which is genuinely
lightweight — this is the cheapest possible shot in the pipeline. But the
reimbursement structure is still unread, and submitting an LOI for something
that may require fronting 100% of costs is not obviously wise. If anyone wants
it, the NGO PDF has to be opened today.

## Realistically closed for this cycle

Saying so plainly rather than carrying them forward as live:

**Second Chance Act, Family-Based — 2026-09-24.** Nonprofits are eligible under
Category 2, and the mission fit is the strongest on the list. But a competitive
federal application in 17 days, with no org-kit content filled, no participant
or outcome data assembled, and no prior federal award, is not a credible
submission. Better to target the next cycle properly than to submit something
thin.

**Second Chance Act, Reentry Education — 2026-09-24.** Likely government-only in
FY26. The county-partner route was the answer, and that window has effectively
closed — counties finalise their own submissions well before the date, so an
approach now arrives too late to be written in.

Both go on the watch list for the next cycle, where they belong. The Family-Based
track in particular is worth preparing for properly: the same application, built
over months with real outcome data, is a genuinely strong bid.
