## The Empower New Jersey fit estimate may be too high

CFNJ's general eligibility is confirmed and favourable: **any 501(c)(3)
nonprofit located in New Jersey is encouraged to apply.** Clear Conscience
Consulting qualifies.

But search describes CFNJ's empowerment-themed funding in economic terms —
economic stability, entrepreneurship, workforce development, financial
literacy, access to opportunity. If "Empower New Jersey" is that program, it is
an **economic-mobility fund, not a behavioral health one.**

That matters because this system has repeatedly called Empower NJ the best
effort-to-probability item on the entire pipeline, and recommended it as the
one thing to write this fall. That recommendation assumed a general community
health scope. If the scope is economic empowerment, the honest fit is lower
than 20%, and the only credible framing would be reentry *employment* rather
than behavioral health education.

**Verify at cfnj.org/find-grants/ before any drafting.** This is a five-minute
check that could redirect the fall's main effort.

## Federal deadlines come in pairs — plan for the earlier one

OJP opportunities observed today carry two deadlines, not one: Grants.gov closes
**two to three days before** JustGrants. Examples seen today: Grants.gov 08/25
with JustGrants 08/27; Grants.gov 08/26 with JustGrants 08/28. The FY25 Second
Chance Act cycle showed the same pattern — Grants.gov May 4, JustGrants May 11.

So a single stated Second Chance Act date is misleading on its face. The
Grants.gov step must be completed first and closes earlier. Whoever confirms
that deadline should capture **both** dates.

The authoritative listing is
`ojp.gov/funding/explore/current-funding-opportunities`.
