## The decision worth making

After 2026-09-30 the near-term calendar empties until November 1. The pipeline
is in good order — 27 opportunities, tiered, with eligibility confirmed on most
near-term items and several corrections caught along the way — but nothing has
been approved, no folder exists, and the org kit's 106 placeholders are
untouched.

Two sensible paths. **Pause this routine** after September 30 and restart it
when someone is ready to work the November items; the pipeline keeps without
maintenance. Or **fill the org kit's high-priority placeholders** — UEI, board
roster, staff credentials, people served, outcome data, budget lines — which is
the one piece of work that unblocks every future application regardless of which
grant gets chosen, and which no amount of searching can substitute for.

Left alone, this routine will keep producing weekly notes against a calendar
with nothing actionable in it. That seems worth avoiding either way.
