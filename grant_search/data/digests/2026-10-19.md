# Grant digest — 2026-10-19

The longest-standing open question in this pipeline is answered, and answering
it surfaced a second HFNJ program worth more than the one we were tracking.

## HFNJ's affiliation question — resolved

Open since 2026-08-19: could a non-Federation-affiliated organization apply
under HFNJ's greater Newark track? Rather than another attempt at the
guidelines, this run looked at **who HFNJ actually funded in 2026**. The answer
is unambiguous — the overwhelming majority are secular community nonprofits:

- **Northwest Essex Community Healthcare Network** — $300,000, coordinated
  behavioral health services for vulnerable youth and families
- **Central Jersey Medical Center** — $105,842, school-based behavioral health
  case manager at two Newark schools
- **Postpartum Support International** — $102,500, perinatal mental health in Newark
- **Visiting Nurse Association of Central NJ** — $127,924, Essex County doula program
- **NJ Chapter, American Academy of Pediatrics** — $90,557
- **Helen Keller International** — $115,000
- **Breakthrough T1D** — $50,000
- **Family Service League** — $100,000 core operating

Only Moving Traditions and JESPY House are Jewish-affiliated. **The greater
Newark track is plainly open.** HFNJ Standard Grants rise from 25% to 30%.

Scale worth knowing: HFNJ awarded roughly **$5 million across the first three
quarters of 2026** — $2.19M in Q1, $686k in Q2, $2.14M across 11 nonprofits in
Q3. An active funder, not a marginal one.

And a useful comparator: Northwest Essex's $300,000 award for *coordinated
behavioral health services for vulnerable youth and families* is the closest
analogue in that list to this organization's work. It validates the fit, and it
names a credible partner or a benchmark competitor.

## The second HFNJ door — $100,000 unrestricted

HFNJ runs a separate **Core Operating Support** initiative: up to $1.5 million
in **unrestricted** grants, with the first 13 grantees receiving **$100,000
each**, and two more added in Q2.

This is worth more to this organization than the Standard Grant it was already
tracking. $100,000 unrestricted is a third of annual budget with no project
restriction — and unrestricted money funds exactly the infrastructure that every
restricted grant quietly assumes already exists: the data systems, the staff
time, the audit. Which is to say, the 106 empty placeholders in the org kit.

Only three items in the entire pipeline offer unrestricted funds, and this is
the largest by a wide margin.

**Open question:** whether it is invitation-only or accepts applications, and
whether another cycle is planned — the first cohort was announced January 2026.
Worth asking in the same conversation as the Standard Grant.

## What stands between here and an application

HFNJ is 31 days out with eligibility confirmed, programmatic fit confirmed, the
funder active, and a close comparator already funded. Nothing external is
blocking it.

What is missing is internal: no folder, no approval, and the org kit's 106
placeholders — UEI, board roster, staff credentials, people served, outcome
data, budget lines. A 31-day runway is comfortable for writing. It is not
comfortable for assembling facts the organization has never written down.
