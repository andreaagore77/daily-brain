# Grant digest — 2026-09-28

The September window has closed. This digest closes the books on it and resets
what the routine is for.

## What passed

**Second Chance Act, both tracks — expired 2026-09-24.** The Grants.gov step
passed on the 24th; JustGrants would have closed October 1, but the first gate
is the binding one. Recorded as closed three weeks ahead, on 2026-09-07, so this
is confirmation rather than news.

**SNAP STEPS LOI — expired 2026-09-12.**

**Empower New Jersey and Champions in Action — two days out**, and neither is
realistically startable now. Empower's scope was never verified; Champions'
date is disputed, with search suggesting that cycle closed in March.

That is five near-term opportunities passing without an application.

## Why, stated plainly

None of them lapsed for lack of fit. The pipeline identified them correctly and
established eligibility on most — the budget band that made Champions viable,
the nonprofit Category 2 lane on the Family-Based track, NJDOL's reimbursement
basis, the CSBG restriction that correctly excluded Affordable Housing.

They lapsed for two reasons. No application was ever started — no folder, no
approval, and the org kit's 106 placeholders untouched, so even an approved
application had nowhere to begin. And the questions that would have settled the
remaining ones each needed a funder's own page, which this sandbox cannot open.

Worth being clear that the second reason is a real limit of this setup, not a
scheduling accident. A weekly automated pass can build and maintain a pipeline.
It cannot open a NOFO, phone a program officer, or know how many people the
organization served last year.

## What October is actually for

The next real deadline is **HFNJ Standard Grants, 2026-11-19 — 52 days out**,
and it is the strongest remaining fit in the pipeline: behavioral health is the
majority of that funder's non-hospital grantmaking, and three of four priority
counties sit in its footprint. One open question, the affiliation clause, and
ample runway.

For that to be winnable, October needs to produce two things this system cannot
generate:

1. **The org kit's high-priority placeholders** — UEI, board roster, staff
   credentials, people served, outcome data, budget lines. Required by every
   application regardless of which one gets chosen.
2. **One phone call to HFNJ** asking whether a non-Federation-affiliated
   organization can apply under the greater Newark track.

With those two done, HFNJ is a straightforward application with seven weeks to
write it. Without them, November repeats September.

## This routine

It stays weekly. I have twice suggested pausing it and will stop suggesting —
the calendar is quiet through October, but HFNJ needs lead time and a pipeline
left entirely unattended is how the November items become December's
retrospective.

October runs will be short. They will check the calendar, watch for new
opportunities, and keep the HFNJ question in front of you. If you would rather
they stop, say so and they will.
